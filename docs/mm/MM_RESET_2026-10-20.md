# MM reset: harness hardening and ceasefire analysis (20 October 2026)

All research processes were stopped before this work. Nothing here placed an order or spent money.

## Why captures stalled

Three captures on 19 October ran about 17 hours past their one-hour schedule with no market data (`lastWireAt` = 0) and no report update after startup. Startup in `scripts/paper-mm.ts` waited on every initial book seed (`await Promise.all(...)`) before starting any timer, with no overall deadline. When that wait never finished, nothing else ran. The SIGTERM handler only cleared a flag, so the processes also ignored a normal stop. Roughly ten captures plus a 24-hour projection were running in parallel, which also produced 15-second seed timeouts across most runs.

## Harness changes (`scripts/paper-mm.ts`)

| Change | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Single-run lock | `tmp/commercial-evidence/.paper-mm.lock`; a second capture refuses to start while the owner is alive. Override with `--allow-concurrent=true`. Stale locks from dead processes are replaced. |
| Startup seed deadline | Initial seeds race a deadline (`--startup-timeout-ms`, default 60 s). Missing it is logged as `startup:initial_seed_deadline_*` and the run continues; seeds keep retrying every second. |
| Market-data watchdog | Fails the run with `startup:no_market_data` if no message arrives within the startup timeout, or `stall:no_market_data_120s` if data stops for two minutes. |
| Hard deadline | Exits with `stall:hard_deadline_exceeded` if the run is still alive 5 minutes plus the startup timeout after its scheduled end, independent of the main loop. |
| Stop signal | SIGTERM/SIGINT stop the run; if shutdown itself hangs, the process exits after 30 s with `forced_exit_after_*`. |
| Book coverage | `report.json` now has `bookCoverage` with market-seconds evaluated, market-seconds with a stale book, and the stale share. |

Tested: a normal 40 s run completed (book coverage 9% stale, lock released); a second run was refused while the first held the lock; SIGTERM on a 600 s run exited in about 3 s with status `interrupted` and the lock released. The no-data and hard-deadline paths were not exercised, because there is no hook to simulate a dead feed.

## Why the ceasefire market "worked"

Source: the corrected 24-hour stub projection (`tmp/commercial-evidence/mm-reward-projection/2026-10-18T22-57-12-060Z`), 25 markets, 70 fills with 60-second markouts, total +$70.10.

| Source of markout | USD |
|---|---|
| US × Iran ceasefire (Oct 31) news shock, 15:53–16:25 UTC: three sells at 0.77 as the midpoint fell to about 0.55, then one buy at 0.62 before a rebound | +$51.35 |
| Cowboys vs. Packers: sells during the live game, as the midpoint went from 0.515 to 0.002 | +$15.59 |
| Everything else, 63 fills across 15 markets | +$3.16 |

- The ceasefire profit is one news shock caught by quotes that refresh only once a minute in the stub. Outside that window, the same market's 16 other fills total about +$2.
- The Cowboys profit came from quoting during a live game. The stub has no event cutoff; the full runner stops quoting 10 minutes before the event, so it would not have taken those fills.
- The second ceasefire market (Oct 22) had 10 fills and −$0.86.
- Without those two artefacts, 24 hours of minimum-size quoting across 25 markets produced about +$3 of 60-second markout on about $3,000 notional, which is essentially flat. Projected maker rewards over the same period were $9.74 per day.

## Stub limitations now confirmed

- Quotes refresh once a minute, so they can sit stale through fast moves.
- No event cutoff, so it quotes into live games.
- Markouts are 60-second marks, not realised P/L; inventory held longer is not tracked and fills are not charged fees.

The stub remains useful for reward-share estimates. It should not be used for trading P/L.

## Candidate next hypothesis

Minimum-size two-sided quotes, refreshed on every book update (full runner, not the stub), in long-dated political markets with deep queues and no scheduled event inside the run, earn maker rewards that exceed adverse selection. Testing this needs reward accrual added to the full runner first, then one 24-hour capture with a predeclared plan, run alone under the new lock.
